### Small tools I've released

Free and open source. All experimental: feedback and issues are welcome in each repo.

- **[Grouped Merge](https://kotijekriv.github.io/grouped-merge/)**: one Word document per spreadsheet group, processed locally in your browser.
- **[KPD Batch Check](https://kotijekriv.github.io/kpd-batch-check/)**: check the KPD 2025 codes in a CSV product catalogue for Croatian e-invoicing, locally in your browser.
- **[Upgrade Check](https://github.com/kotijekriv/upgrade-check)**: a read-only QGIS addon that reports what official repositories declare about your installed plugins before an upgrade.
- **[One Cook](https://kotijekriv.github.io/one-cook/)**: plan several dishes backwards from one serving time and see where your hands, oven or hob are needed twice, locally in your browser.
- **[PPTX Font Locator](https://kotijekriv.github.io/pptx-font-locator/)**: find where a font is stored in a PowerPoint file, down to the slide, master, list level and XML path, locally in your browser.
